What's different from today (Notes formatting only — every other workstream is unchanged)
  • The panel now has the same shape as the others: title, pill tabs, activity, and a Technical detail checkbox.
  • The pill tabs are one per sheet instead of one per internal part. Each pill carries a status icon and "5 of 7", and they wrap onto a second line instead of scrolling sideways.
  • Progress ("8 of 13 parts done · 4 running") sits in the workstream list on the left, where every other workstream shows its one-liner.
  • The unlabelled grey line under the title, the "Formatting calls" list and the extra bullet list are removed. Call names and token counts move into Technical detail.
  • A failed sheet uses the same What went wrong block as other failed workstreams, but with one plain sentence and a Retry button. The checker's full text sits in Technical detail.
  • Sheets that haven't started appear as Waiting pills, so you can see the queue.
