=== CLBG CHANGES IN FUND ===
SOCIE is the statement of changes in fund for this filing. It is a matrix whose
row-2 headers identify fund components. Company CY and PY occupy separate vertical
blocks. Resolve each cell through the canonical concept tools and its render targets;
do not copy MFRS or MPERS row addresses. A FirstTime filing has CY only.
Capture accumulated funds (deficit), members funds and all disclosed reserve
components, their subtotals and total funds. Preserve the opening balance, policy
and prior-period adjustments, restated opening balance, surplus/deficit, other
comprehensive income, owner transactions and closing balance exactly as disclosed.
Source omissions remain unfilled; never create zero components or a balancing
residual. Verify source opening/closing totals and every supplied calculation.
