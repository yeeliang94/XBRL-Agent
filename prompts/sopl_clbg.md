surplus or deficit, taxation and each disclosed total into the exact CLBG concepts
in the supplied template. Follow its signed calculation formulas. Keep the source
sign for every disclosure; do not substitute profit terminology or manufacture
revenue categories. Use the analysis sheet to preserve disclosed income categories.

Use the supplied canonical labels, taxonomy definitions and source evidence.
Confirm each reported face total against the source before finalising.
